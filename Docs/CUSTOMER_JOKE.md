# Customer Case — The Skibidi Mooncake of Eternal Rizz

**Tone:** Intentionally unbearable cringe comedy; short bonus case  
**Customer:** Kai “SigmaKaito_GYATT” Tan, a catastrophically online teenager  
**Story purpose:** Provide comic relief while using the same deduction mechanics.

## Arrival

Kai slides sideways into the bakery while filming himself. He wears sunglasses indoors, a plastic crown labelled **RIZZ KING**, and a hoodie reading **I PAUSED MY GRINDSET TO BE HERE**. His phone plays three notification sounds at once.

He needs a mooncake for someone he has been speaking to online for eleven days. He describes this as a “long-term interdimensional situationship” and insists the bakery treat it with “maximum emotional aura.”

## Customer dialogue

**Kai:** “Chat, we have located the bakery. W establishment. Baker. Cat. Lock in. I require your most romantically goated mooncake. Maximum aura. Infinite rizz. No Ohio ingredients.”

**Baker:** “For an anniversary?”

**Kai:** “Way deeper. We have maintained an eleven-day Snap streak. Basically married in the digital realm. Our bond is low-key high-key canon.”

Artemis slowly looks toward the camera. A dialogue option appears that only says **[Endure]**.

**Kai:** “My e-kitten—respectfully, platonically, romantically, it's complicated—goes by LunarWolfPrincess_xX. She says red is the colour of our shared aura because our love is giving crimson main-character energy. So the filling must be red. This is crucial lore.”

**Baker:** “Red bean?”

**Kai:** “Bro! No spoilers! Let the cat cook. You just leaked the answer before the season finale. Actual NPC behaviour.”

**Kai:** “Also, absolutely zero egg yolk. She says yolks have negative aura, destroy the jawline, and are an immediate unfollow. If a yolk touches this mooncake, my relationship gets fanum-taxed into the shadow realm.”

**Baker:** “I wish I didn't.”

**Kai:** “Sweetness must be maxed. Full send. She says life is already bitter when your ranked teammates are hard-stuck bronze, your Wi-Fi has no rizz, and your mum tells you to take the bins out during the final circle.”

Artemis covers her face with both paws. The moon visibly dims.

**Kai:** “For the finishing buff, sesame. We both got sesame in a ‘Which Tiny Seed Matches Your Freak?’ quiz. Ninety-eight percent compatibility. The missing two percent is because I'm an asparagus moon and she's a microwave rising.”

**Baker:** “And have you met this person?”

**Kai:** “Emotionally? Every night in the Discord VC.”

**Baker:** “In person?”

**Kai:** “Face-to-face interaction is legacy content. Our souls have already synced in the metaverse. Please respect the grind.”

**Baker:** “What is her actual name?”

**Kai:** “Names are government usernames.”

Artemis gets up and walks three steps away despite the game having no movement system.

## Artemis's memory notes

- “Crimson main-character aura means the filling must be red. Somehow.”
- “Egg yolks possess negative aura and would send the relationship to the shadow realm.”
- “Maximum sweetness counters bronze teammates, weak Wi-Fi, and household chores.”
- “Sesame allegedly matches their freak at ninety-eight percent compatibility.”

## Puzzle solution

| Recipe category | Correct choice | Story evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Filling | Red bean | The filling must match their “red aura” |
| Centre | No yolk | LunarWolfPrincess dislikes egg yolks |
| Sweetness | Regular/extra sweet | Kai explicitly asks for extra sweetness |
| Finish | Sesame | Their dubious compatibility quiz chose sesame |

If the preparation screen labels the option **Regular** rather than **Extra Sweet**, Regular should remain the sweeter of the two available choices.

## Decoy choices

- Lotus filling
- Salted egg yolk
- Low sweetness
- Osmanthus finish

## Wrong-answer feedback

- **Wrong filling:** Kai says, “That filling has beige NPC aura. It's not giving crimson protagonist. It's giving unskippable tutorial character.”
- **Wrong centre:** Kai gasps and says, “A yolk? In this economy? You are about to get me blocked, ratioed, unfollowed, and emotionally sent to Ohio.”
- **Wrong sweetness:** He tastes it, removes his sunglasses, and whispers, “This cake is rizzless. Cooked. Absolutely beta. Even my Wi-Fi has more flavour.”
- **Wrong finish:** He checks the quiz again and says, “Osmanthus does not match our freak. My soul is a sesame seed with abandonment issues. Respectfully.”

After a second failed attempt, Artemis pushes the relevant memory note to the centre of the counter while maintaining direct eye contact with the player.

## Correct-recipe memory reveal

The mold glows with far more drama than the situation deserves. Bass-boosted celestial music plays. Glittering words fly across the screen:

> **LEGENDARY MOONCAKE**  
> **+9,999 RELATIONSHIP AURA**  
> **RIZZ MULTIPLIER: GYATT**

A sparkly vertical-video memory appears with fake comments floating beside it. Kai and LunarWolfPrincess_xX sit on opposite sides of a video call wearing matching wolf-ear filters. They take the same ridiculous personality quiz, both receive sesame, and yell “W seed!” at exactly the same time.

They perform half of a synchronized dance, lose connection, reconnect, and say “skibidi” several times without context. Eventually, the filters fall away and they admit that both of them felt lonely during the festival and were afraid to say so normally.

Beneath the embarrassing language, the connection is genuine. The joke is Kai's presentation, not his loneliness.

## Resolution dialogue

**Kai:** “Chat. CHAT. This mooncake is absolutely bussin. Michelin rizz. Our lore is officially edible. The bakery cooked, served, devoured, and left zero crumbs. Massive W.”

His phone buzzes.

**Kai:** “NO SHOT. LunarWolfPrincess_xX says she lives two streets away. The long-distance arc was filler this whole time.”

**Baker:** “Then perhaps you could meet and speak without the usernames.”

**Kai:** “Whoa. Face reveal? Grass touching? In this patch? Let's not speedrun the relationship, unc.”

Kai attempts to dab, knocks his crown off, says “calculated,” and leaves with the mooncake while livestreaming the bakery door. Artemis stares after him, spiritually exhausted. The baker silently places a tiny plain biscuit in front of her. It has no filling, no finish, and no lore.
