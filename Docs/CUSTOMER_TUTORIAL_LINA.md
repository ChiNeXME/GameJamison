# Tutorial Customer — Lina Chen

**Role:** First customer and guided recipe tutorial

**Emotional purpose:** Introduce the idea that adapting a tradition can be an act of care.

Lina has returned home after five years away. She wants to bring her estranged father a mooncake based on the version he quietly adapted for her as a child. The cake will not repair their relationship by itself; it gives Lina the courage to knock on his door and begin speaking.

## Tutorial recipe

| Step | Correct choice | Clear story clue | Mechanic taught |
|---|---|---|---|
| Filling | Red bean paste | Lina stole warm red bean paste from her father's spoon | Drag/select a filling |
| Centre | Leave empty | Her father omitted the yolk she disliked | Deliberately choose no centre |
| Sweetness | Less sugar | He reduced the sugar after listening to her | Use the sweetness control |
| Finish | Osmanthus | A tiny flower marked her personal cake | Drag/select a finish |

## Tutorial flow

1. Reveal one recipe category at a time.
2. The Baker explains the type of choice in one sentence.
3. Artemis's notebook displays the matching clue.
4. Highlight only the relevant controls until the player chooses.
5. Give immediate confirmation and unlock the next category.
6. Unlock the mold only after all four choices are made.
7. A wrong choice triggers Lina's direct reminder; tutorial mistakes carry no penalty.

After Lina leaves, remove the category-by-category guidance. Mei and Jian require the player to review all four memories and assemble the recipe independently.
