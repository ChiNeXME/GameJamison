# Grandmother — Dialogue

Grandmother appears only in remembered audio and the revealed memory. Her remembered lines may begin muffled, becoming clear after the correct recipe.

- **S2-08 — Mei's memory:** Slowly, Mei. Smooth paste comes from patience, not force.
- **S2-10 — Mei's memory:** Sweetness should carry the lotus, not bury it.
- **S3-12 — Jian's memory:** Gently, Jian. Set the little moon exactly in the middle.
- **S6-02:** Slowly, Mei. Let the stone do the work.
- **S6-04:** Jian's part is different from yours. Different is not the same as easier.
- **S6-05:** One spoonful of sugar. We should taste the lotus first.
- **S6-06:** Now, Jian. Your little moon.
- **S6-08:** Then Mei will help you straighten it.
- **S6-12:** Good. A family recipe is too heavy for one pair of hands.
- **S6-14:** There. One cake, both of you.
- **S6-16:** One day, you'll make this without me.

## Performance

Affectionate, practical, and never saintly or overly solemn. “One day, you'll make this without me” is gentle and matter-of-fact; the sadness comes from the present-day context.
