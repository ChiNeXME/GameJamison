# Lina Chen — Dialogue

Lina is the first customer and the guided gameplay tutorial. She is a florist who returned home after five years away and wants to approach her estranged father. Her correct recipe is red bean paste, no yolk, less sugar, and osmanthus. Present her clues in the same order as the four recipe controls, unlocking each control after its clue.

- **L1-03:** Five. I kept finding reasons not to come home.
- **L1-05:** I ran out of reasons that sounded honest.
- **L1-07:** My father left this at my flower shop this morning. He didn't come inside.
- **L1-08:** We haven't spoken properly since I left. I want to bring him one of the mooncakes he used to make for me. But I never learned the recipe.
- **L2-01:** Red bean paste. I used to steal warm spoonfuls while he was filling the molds.
- **L2-03:** No salted yolk. I hated the salty centre, so he quietly stopped adding one to mine.
- **L2-05:** Grandfather thought changing a family recipe was disrespectful. Dad never argued. He just marked my cakes with a tiny flower so I would know.
- **L2-07:** He also cut back the sugar after I said the filling made my teeth ache. I thought he was being stingy.
- **L2-08:** It took me years to understand how closely he had been listening.
- **L2-10:** Osmanthus. He dried petals from the tree outside our house and pressed them over the top.
- **L2-12:** I don't expect one cake to fix five silent years. I only want it to say that I'm ready to knock on his door.
- **L3-F1:** No, it was darker and warmer. Red bean paste.
- **L3-C1:** Dad always left the centre of mine empty.
- **L3-S1:** He changed it because the old filling was too sweet for me.
- **L3-O1:** The flower was his way of telling my cake apart.
- **L4-02:** He changed a family recipe for me, and I mistook his quietness for not caring.
- **L4-04:** This won't undo what I said before I left.
- **L4-06:** Then I should go before I invent another excuse.

## Performance

Quietly confident in ordinary conversation, but careful and vulnerable when speaking about her father. Her resolution is a decision to attempt reconciliation, not proof that reconciliation has already happened.
