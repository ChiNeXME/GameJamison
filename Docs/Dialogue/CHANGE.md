# Chang'e — Dialogue

Chang'e is heard through Artemis's fragmented divine memory. Use a soft, clear voice with a slight distant quality.

- **S1-06 — Fragment:** When people forget, help them remember.
- **S8-02:** Artemis, even the moon disappears from sight.
- **S8-04:** They remember. They tell one another. They keep a place for it at the table.
- **S8-05:** To remember someone is not to keep them unchanged. It is to carry what remains, knowing something will always be missing.

## Performance

Calm and intimate, as if speaking to one trusted companion rather than addressing an audience. The final line should feel consoling without denying loss.
