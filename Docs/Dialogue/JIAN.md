# Jian — Dialogue

Jian arrives later and separately, looking for the family tin. He must not already be present in Mei's scene.

- **S3-02:** There it is. I've been looking everywhere for that tin.
- **S3-04:** I hoped it would be here.
- **S3-07:** And she told you I lost the recipe.
- **S3-09:** I said it might have been red bean because I was guessing. I lost the page while we were clearing Grandmother's house.
- **S3-09B:** I was ashamed. By the time I admitted it to myself, I didn't know how to admit it to you.
- **S3-11:** One salted yolk in the centre. Grandmother let me place it there.
- **S3-13:** I was terrified I'd break it. She guided my hands until it sat perfectly straight.
- **S3-16:** Because you were on the other side of the table, fighting the grinder.
- **S3-18:** You told a kitchen tool it had insulted your honour.
- **S3-20:** So was I. Twins, remember?
- **S3-23:** And the flowers. Osmanthus. She scattered them over the mold because Mei liked the smell.
- **S3-25:** I remember you sneezing into the flour.
- **S5-C1:** Something's missing. Grandmother's little moon belonged in the middle.
- **S5-O2:** Osmanthus. It used to stick to our sleeves.
- **S6-07 — Young Jian:** What if I put it in crooked?
- **S6-10 — Young Jian:** Please, honoured grinder warrior.
- **S7-03:** I thought you had decided my part didn't matter.
- **S7-05:** I did lose the page. I'm sorry. I thought losing it meant I had failed her—and you.
- **S7-08:** It tastes like her kitchen.
- **S7-10:** Almost is more than I thought we had left.
- **S7-14:** I'll keep the recipe. Properly this time.

## Performance

Defensive energy covers shame about losing the written recipe and fear that his memories do not count. His apology should be plain and unguarded.
