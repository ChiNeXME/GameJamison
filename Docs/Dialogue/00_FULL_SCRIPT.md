# Full Dialogue Script

This is the chronological assembly script. Character-only recording sheets use the same IDs. Lina's story is Customer 1; the twins' story follows as the emotional finale.

## S1 — Opening

**[S1-01 | Stage]** The Mid-Autumn moon hangs pale behind the bakery window. The Baker discovers Artemis curled beside an old, cracked mooncake mold.

**[S1-02 | Baker]** There you are. I wondered who kept sleeping beneath my window.

**[S1-03 | Baker]** You chose a strange night to visit. Even the moon seems to have forgotten how to shine.

**[S1-04 | Artemis — thought]** That mold... Why does it feel familiar?

**[S1-05 | Stage]** Artemis touches the mold. A thread of moonlight passes through its crack.

**[S1-06 | Chang'e — memory, faint]** When people forget, help them remember.

**[S1-07 | Artemis — thought]** Who said that?

**[S1-08 | Baker]** Hm? Did you hear something too, little one?

## L1 — First customer: Lina arrives

**[L1-01 | Stage]** The bell rings. Lina enters carrying a worn square mooncake box tied with a faded yellow ribbon.

**[L1-02 | Baker]** Lina Chen. How many festivals has it been?

**[L1-03 | Lina]** Five. I kept finding reasons not to come home.

**[L1-04 | Baker]** And this year?

**[L1-05 | Lina]** I ran out of reasons that sounded honest.

**[L1-06 | Stage]** She places the empty box on the counter.

**[L1-07 | Lina]** My father left this at my flower shop this morning. He didn't come inside.

**[L1-08 | Lina]** We haven't spoken properly since I left. I want to bring him one of the mooncakes he used to make for me—but I never learned the recipe.

**[L1-09 | Baker]** Tell us what you do remember.

## L2 — Lina's clues

**[L2-01 | Lina]** Red bean paste. I used to steal warm spoonfuls while he was filling the molds.

**[L2-02 | Artemis — thought]** Red bean. A memory she can name without hesitation.

**[L2-03 | Lina]** No salted yolk. I hated the salty centre, so he quietly stopped adding one to mine.

**[L2-04 | Baker]** Quietly?

**[L2-05 | Lina]** Grandfather thought changing a family recipe was disrespectful. Dad never argued. He just marked my cakes with a tiny flower so I would know.

**[L2-06 | Artemis — thought]** Leave the centre empty.

**[L2-07 | Lina]** He also cut back the sugar after I said the filling made my teeth ache. I thought he was being stingy.

**[L2-08 | Lina]** It took me years to understand how closely he had been listening.

**[L2-09 | Baker]** And the flower mark?

**[L2-10 | Lina]** Osmanthus. He dried petals from the tree outside our house and pressed them over the top.

**[L2-11 | Artemis — thought]** Red bean. No yolk. Less sugar. Osmanthus.

**[L2-12 | Lina]** I don't expect one cake to fix five silent years. I only want it to say that I'm ready to knock on his door.

## L3 — Lina recipe prompt

This first recipe is the guided tutorial. Unlock one category at a time and let the Baker explain the interaction before the player chooses.

**[L3-00A | Baker]** We begin with the filling. Choose the paste Lina remembers most clearly.

**[L3-01 | Artemis — notebook]** Lina stole warm red bean paste from her father's spoon.

**[L3-01A | Tutorial]** Highlight the filling choices. The player selects or drags **Red bean paste**.

**[L3-01B | Baker]** Now the centre. Sometimes the important choice is what someone deliberately left out.

**[L3-02 | Artemis — notebook]** Her father left out the yolk she disliked.

**[L3-02A | Tutorial]** Highlight the centre control. The player selects **Leave empty**.

**[L3-02B | Baker]** Sweetness is a recipe setting, not an ingredient. What did her father change?

**[L3-03 | Artemis — notebook]** He reduced the sugar because he listened.

**[L3-03A | Tutorial]** Highlight the sweetness control. The player selects **Less sugar**.

**[L3-03B | Baker]** Last comes the finish—the mark that told Lina the cake was hers.

**[L3-04 | Artemis — notebook]** A tiny osmanthus flower marked the cake as hers.

**[L3-04A | Tutorial]** Highlight the finish choices. The player selects or drags **Osmanthus**, then the mold button unlocks.

**[L3-05 | Baker]** Good. Press the mold when the four memories agree.

### Lina wrong filling

**[L3-F1 | Lina]** No, it was darker and warmer. Red bean paste.

**[L3-F2 | Artemis — direct hint, second miss]** Use red bean paste.

### Lina wrong centre

**[L3-C1 | Lina]** Dad always left the centre of mine empty.

**[L3-C2 | Artemis — direct hint, second miss]** Leave out the salted yolk.

### Lina wrong sweetness

**[L3-S1 | Lina]** He changed it because the old filling was too sweet for me.

**[L3-S2 | Artemis — direct hint, second miss]** Choose less sugar.

### Lina wrong finish

**[L3-O1 | Lina]** The flower was his way of telling my cake apart.

**[L3-O2 | Artemis — direct hint, second miss]** Finish with osmanthus.

## L4 — Lina resolution

**[L4-01 | Stage]** The finished mooncake bears a small osmanthus flower. Lina turns the box in her hands but does not open it immediately.

**[L4-02 | Lina]** He changed a family recipe for me, and I mistook his quietness for not caring.

**[L4-03 | Baker]** You were young. So was the hurt.

**[L4-04 | Lina]** This won't undo what I said before I left.

**[L4-05 | Baker]** No. But it can accompany what you say next.

**[L4-06 | Lina]** Then I should go before I invent another excuse.

**[L4-07 | Stage]** Lina ties the yellow ribbon, thanks Artemis with a small bow, and leaves carrying the mooncake to her father's house.

**[L4-08 | Artemis — thought]** A changed recipe can still carry its roots.

**[L4-09 | Stage]** The moon brightens slightly. One deeper memory remains out of reach. The bell rings again: Mei has arrived.

## S2 — Mei arrives alone

**[S2-01 | Stage]** The bell rings. Mei enters alone, holding a dented mooncake tin.

**[S2-02 | Baker]** Welcome. What can I make for you?

**[S2-03 | Mei]** I'm hoping you can remake something. My grandmother's mooncake.

**[S2-04 | Baker]** From a written recipe?

**[S2-05 | Mei]** I wish. Grandmother died last winter. Jian was meant to keep the recipe, but now he says he cannot find it.

**[S2-06 | Baker]** Then tell me what you remember.

**[S2-07 | Mei]** Lotus. I used to grind the soaked seeds while Grandmother watched.

**[S2-08 | Grandmother — remembered]** Slowly, Mei. Smooth paste comes from patience, not force.

**[S2-09 | Mei]** She added one small spoonful of sugar. Never more.

**[S2-10 | Grandmother — remembered]** Sweetness should carry the lotus, not bury it.

**[S2-11 | Artemis — thought]** Lotus paste. Only a little sugar.

**[S2-12 | Baker]** Anything at the centre?

**[S2-13 | Mei]** No. At least... I don't remember one. Jian insists there was a yolk, but he remembers the things he ate, not the things he made.

**[S2-14 | Baker]** Not remembering is different from remembering there was none.

**[S2-15 | Mei]** You sound like Grandmother.

**[S2-16 | Baker]** I'll take that as a compliment.

**[S2-17 | Mei]** There was a floral smell. It clung to her sleeves and the paper around every cake.

**[S2-18 | Baker]** Jasmine?

**[S2-19 | Mei]** Osmanthus. She tucked a sprig behind my ear while the cakes cooled.

**[S2-20 | Artemis — thought]** Osmanthus. The mold warmed when she said it.

**[S2-21 | Mei]** This is our first festival without her. I thought if it tasted the same, the house might feel less empty.

## S3 — Jian arrives separately

**[S3-01 | Stage]** The bell rings again. Jian enters, slightly out of breath. He stops when he sees Mei and the tin.

**[S3-02 | Jian]** There it is. I've been looking everywhere for that tin.

**[S3-03 | Mei]** It was in the cupboard where you left it three years ago.

**[S3-04 | Jian]** I hoped it would be here.

**[S3-05 | Mei]** By forgetting it existed?

**[S3-06 | Baker]** You must be Jian.

**[S3-07 | Jian]** And she told you I lost the recipe.

**[S3-08 | Mei]** You said red bean last week.

**[S3-09 | Jian]** I said it might have been red bean because I was guessing. I lost the page while we were clearing Grandmother's house.

**[S3-09A | Mei]** You told me it was somewhere safe.

**[S3-09B | Jian]** I was ashamed. By the time I admitted it to myself, I didn't know how to admit it to you.

**[S3-10 | Baker]** Which was?

**[S3-11 | Jian]** One salted yolk in the centre. Grandmother let me place it there.

**[S3-12 | Grandmother — remembered]** Gently, Jian. Set the little moon exactly in the middle.

**[S3-13 | Jian]** I was terrified I'd break it. She guided my hands until it sat perfectly straight.

**[S3-14 | Artemis — thought]** One yolk. A little moon in the middle.

**[S3-15 | Mei]** I don't remember that.

**[S3-16 | Jian]** Because you were on the other side of the table, fighting the grinder.

**[S3-17 | Mei]** I was not fighting it.

**[S3-18 | Jian]** You told a kitchen tool it had insulted your honour.

**[S3-19 | Mei]** I was eight.

**[S3-20 | Jian]** So was I. Twins, remember?

**[S3-21 | Stage]** Mei almost smiles. The tension softens.

**[S3-22 | Baker]** Mei remembers the lotus and the small spoonful of sugar. Jian remembers the yolk. Both of you remember helping at the same table.

**[S3-23 | Jian]** And the flowers. Osmanthus. She scattered them over the mold because Mei liked the smell.

**[S3-24 | Mei]** You remember that too?

**[S3-25 | Jian]** I remember you sneezing into the flour.

**[S3-26 | Mei]** Once.

**[S3-27 | Baker]** Perhaps the recipe was never kept by one person.

**[S3-28 | Artemis — thought]** Each carried a different half. Perhaps what remains is enough.

## S4 — Recipe prompt

**[S4-01 | Baker]** Little helper, shall we see what their memories make together?

**[S4-02 | Artemis — notebook]** Mei ground lotus seeds beside Grandmother.

**[S4-03 | Artemis — notebook]** One small spoonful—the sugar must not bury the lotus.

**[S4-04 | Artemis — notebook]** Jian placed one little moon in the centre.

**[S4-05 | Artemis — notebook]** Osmanthus clung to Grandmother's sleeves and finished the cakes.

## S5 — Wrong recipe feedback

Play one applicable branch, then return to the recipe board.

### Wrong filling

**[S5-F1 | Baker]** This filling doesn't match the memory of the grinder.

**[S5-F2 | Mei]** I can still feel those lotus seeds slipping beneath the stone.

**[S5-F3 | Artemis — direct hint, second miss]** The filling was lotus paste.

### Wrong centre

**[S5-C1 | Jian]** Something's missing. Grandmother's little moon belonged in the middle.

**[S5-C2 | Baker]** A memory that precise deserves a place in the recipe.

**[S5-C3 | Artemis — direct hint, second miss]** Place one salted egg yolk in the centre.

### Wrong sweetness

**[S5-S1 | Mei]** That's sweeter than hers. The sugar is hiding the lotus.

**[S5-S2 | Baker]** Then we use a lighter hand.

**[S5-S3 | Artemis — direct hint, second miss]** Choose less sugar.

### Wrong finish

**[S5-O1 | Mei]** The scent is wrong. Grandmother smelled like the tree outside her kitchen.

**[S5-O2 | Jian]** Osmanthus. It used to stick to our sleeves.

**[S5-O3 | Artemis — direct hint, second miss]** Finish the cake with osmanthus.

## S6 — Correct recipe and revealed memory

**[S6-01 | Stage]** The mold fills with pale gold light. The bakery dissolves into Grandmother's kitchen years earlier.

**[S6-02 | Grandmother]** Slowly, Mei. Let the stone do the work.

**[S6-03 | Young Mei]** Jian finished his part already.

**[S6-04 | Grandmother]** Jian's part is different from yours. Different is not the same as easier.

**[S6-05 | Grandmother]** One spoonful of sugar. We should taste the lotus first.

**[S6-06 | Grandmother]** Now, Jian—your little moon.

**[S6-07 | Young Jian]** What if I put it in crooked?

**[S6-08 | Grandmother]** Then Mei will help you straighten it.

**[S6-09 | Young Mei]** Only if he asks nicely.

**[S6-10 | Young Jian]** Please, honoured grinder warrior.

**[S6-11 | Young Mei]** Accepted.

**[S6-12 | Grandmother]** Good. A family recipe is too heavy for one pair of hands.

**[S6-13 | Stage]** Grandmother guides both children as they close the lotus paste around the yolk, dust the mold with osmanthus, and press together.

**[S6-14 | Grandmother]** There. One cake, both of you.

**[S6-15 | Stage]** Grandmother sets the cake on the windowsill and looks at the twins for a long moment.

**[S6-16 | Grandmother]** One day, you'll make this without me.

**[S6-17 | Stage]** The memory fades before either child can answer.

## S7 — Resolution

**[S7-01 | Stage]** The bakery returns. Mei and Jian look at the finished mooncake.

**[S7-02 | Mei]** I thought you were changing her recipe to hide that you lost it.

**[S7-03 | Jian]** I thought you had decided my part didn't matter.

**[S7-04 | Mei]** I couldn't remember your part, so I treated it as if it never happened.

**[S7-05 | Jian]** I did lose the page. I'm sorry. I thought losing it meant I had failed her—and you.

**[S7-06 | Mei]** I blamed you because being angry was easier than saying she's gone.

**[S7-07 | Stage]** They split the mooncake and taste it. For a moment, neither speaks.

**[S7-08 | Jian]** It tastes like her kitchen.

**[S7-09 | Mei]** Almost.

**[S7-10 | Jian]** Almost is more than I thought we had left.

**[S7-11 | Stage]** The Baker places a blank recipe card between them. Mei writes the filling and sweetness. Jian adds the centre and finish.

**[S7-12 | Baker]** It may not be every word she wrote. It is what the two of you can carry forward.

**[S7-13 | Mei]** I'll keep the tin.

**[S7-14 | Jian]** I'll keep the recipe. Properly this time.

**[S7-15 | Mei]** We'll both keep copies.

**[S7-16 | Stage]** They leave together. Mei carries the old tin; Jian carries the new recipe.

## S8 — Artemis and Chang'e ending

**[S8-01 | Stage]** Moonlight pours through the window. The restored glow of the mold becomes another memory.

**[S8-02 | Chang'e]** Artemis, even the moon disappears from sight.

**[S8-03 | Artemis — memory]** Then how do people know it will return?

**[S8-04 | Chang'e]** They remember. They tell one another. They keep a place for it at the table.

**[S8-05 | Chang'e]** To remember someone is not to keep them unchanged. It is to carry what remains, knowing something will always be missing.

**[S8-06 | Artemis — thought]** Chang'e. I remember your name.

**[S8-07 | Stage]** The memory fades. The Baker sets a tiny cat-shaped mooncake on the windowsill.

**[S8-08 | Baker]** Every helper deserves a share.

**[S8-09 | Artemis — thought]** The past does not return. It leaves us enough light to follow.

**[S8-10 | Stage]** Artemis takes a bite. The moon grows brighter, but does not become full. The mold glows while its crack remains visible. Fade to title and credits.
